WhatsApp Patient Assistant — Proposal

An AI helper on WhatsApp that runs your clinic's front desk.

It books appointments, answers common patient questions, sends reminders, and follows up — all by itself, all on WhatsApp. Fewer missed calls, fewer empty slots.

What your clinic is losing today

Missed calls

You're with a patient, phone rings, no one picks up — that person may not call again.

Empty slots

Patient forgets the appointment because no one reminded them.

Manual booking

Staff juggles calls and a register — double-bookings happen.

No follow-up

After a visit, calling every patient back depends on staff remembering.

What the WhatsApp Assistant does

  • Books appointments, any time of dayPatient messages the clinic number, bot checks open slots, books it — even at 11 PM.
  • Sends reminders automaticallyA WhatsApp message a day before and a few hours before — so fewer patients forget.
  • Answers common questions instantlyFasting instructions before endoscopy, clinic timings, fees, directions — patient gets the answer without calling.
  • Follows up after the visitChecks in after a procedure or consult, and reminds regular patients when it's time to come back.
  • Asks happy patients for a reviewAfter a good visit, it asks for a Google review — helps your clinic show up better online.
  • Understands English, Hindi, and HinglishPatients can type however they naturally would — the bot understands mixed language messages, no fixed menu needed.
  • One simple screen for you and staffEvery conversation, booking, and reminder in one place. Patients don't install anything new — they just use WhatsApp.
